Privacy Policy

Antar Marg · Last updated: August 30, 2026

Draft for review. This is not legal advice. Have a lawyer (or at least a careful human read) before hosting this at a public URL or submitting to App Store Connect.

1. Who we are

This policy describes how Antar Marg (“the App”) collects and uses information. The App is a Hindu/Vedic daily-practice, library, and sanctuary experience for iOS.

2. What we collect

  • Account. If you sign in with Google or Sign in with Apple, we receive the identifier and the name/email those providers share with us. We store the account in Supabase Auth.
  • Practice and progress. Daily tasks, habits, streaks, reading progress, bookmarks, notes, journey progress, and sanctuary customizations are stored in Supabase (and cached on-device) so they persist across sessions.
  • Purchases. Subscription status and transaction identifiers are processed by Apple and RevenueCat. We do not receive your full payment card number.
  • Push token. If you allow notifications, we store a device push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging / APNs) so we can send the reminders you opted into.
  • Diagnostics. Crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics (release builds) and funnel events via Firebase Analytics: onboarding complete, paywall viewed, purchase completed, tab viewed. Events use your Supabase user id only — not your email or name.
  • On-device preferences. Language, sound, and notification toggles stay on the device.

We do not operate a live AI chatbot. Verse “AI commentary” is pre-written text stored in our database, not a live model call from your device.

3. Why we use it

To run the App (auth, sync, purchases), send notifications you enable, fix crashes, understand which screens people reach (in aggregate), and comply with law. We do not sell personal information to data brokers or advertisers.

4. Who we share it with (processors)

  • Supabase — authentication, database, storage
  • Firebase (Google) — push, Crashlytics, Analytics
  • RevenueCat — subscription entitlements
  • Apple / Google — sign-in and App Store billing

These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect safety.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep account and progress data while your account exists. You can sign out anytime; signing out clears this device’s cached user data. To delete an account, contact the support email on the App Store listing. Some records may be retained where the law requires it (e.g. purchase receipts).

6. Your choices

You can refuse sign-in (limited local use), disable notifications, and manage subscriptions in iOS Settings. Depending on your region you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data — contact us to exercise them.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum digital age in your region). We do not knowingly collect their personal information.

8. International transfers

Supabase, Firebase, and RevenueCat may process data in the United States or other countries where they operate.

9. Changes

We will update the “Last updated” date when this policy changes. Material changes may also be noted in an App Store release.

10. Contact

Use the support contact on the App Store listing or the email published on antarmarg.app.
